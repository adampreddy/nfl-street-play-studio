# NFL Street Play Studio

Make your own plays for NFL Street 2 and try them in NFL Street Reborn.

## Download

Get the latest zip from [Releases](../../releases/latest). Unzip it anywhere you can write to (Desktop or
Documents) and open `NFL Street Play Studio.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose More info > Run anyway.

## What you need

- Windows
- Your own copy of NFL Street 2. Play Studio finds NFL Street Reborn on its own; without Reborn it asks for
  your NFL Street 2 GameCube ISO. It reads the original plays from your game the first time it starts.
- NFL Street Reborn, to play your plays in the game (Play > Play in Reborn)

This download contains no game files.

## Updates

Play Studio checks this page when it starts and offers each new version. Click Update and it swaps itself in.
Your plays and settings are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\NFL Street Play Studio`, so updates never touch them.
